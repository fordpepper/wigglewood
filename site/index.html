How Wigglewood is doing

What is this?

Wigglewood is a little island that never stops. Dumplings eat berries, grumbles eat dumplings, and both have babies that inherit their parents' genes with small changes. Over time the island figures out what works.

It's the same world for everyone. The whole island is worked out from the current time, so if you and a friend open it at the same moment you'll see the same creatures in the same places. Close the tab and come back tomorrow: life carried on without you.

The sky follows your own clock, so it's night here when it's night where you are. Seasons belong to the island: each one lasts three hours.

Looking around

  • Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Click a creature to see its genes, family and mood.
  • Arrow keys or WASD pan, + and − zoom, F follows, Esc lets go.

The genes

  • Size: bigger stores more food and is harder to catch, but burns energy fast.
  • Speed: outrun or run down, at a cost.
  • Sight: spot food and danger from further away.
  • Colour, eyes, patterns and hats drift over generations, so you can see families.

Every 30 minutes a GitHub Action moves the island forward and saves a checkpoint; your browser picks it up and simulates the last few minutes itself.
